### Added
- Initial release.
- `generate_key`, `key_for`, `encrypt`, `decrypt`, `describe_key`.
- `symbols_to_text`, `text_to_symbols` low-level converters.
- Pure-text (hex-token) serialization for keys and ciphertexts.
